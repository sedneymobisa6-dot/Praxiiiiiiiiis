When Someone Asks
When a person
asks for their data,
you must respond.
Every person has the right to see, correct, or delete the data you hold about them. The law gives you a set time to respond. Ignoring the request is not an option.
If you refuse or delay without reason, the ODPC can order you to compensate the person for the harm caused. This is not a fine paid to the government. It is money paid directly to the individual whose rights you violated.
Section 65, Data Protection Act, 2019.

When Data Leaks
If your data
is breached,
you have 72 hours.
If your systems are hacked, or personal data is lost, stolen, or exposed, you must tell the ODPC within 72 hours. You must also inform the people affected.
Failing to report a breach is a serious offence. The penalty is up to KES 5,000,000 or 1% of your annual revenue. And the people affected can still sue you for compensation separately.
Section 43, Data Protection Act, 2019.
