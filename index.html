Your Obligations
The clock starts
the moment you
receive a request.
01
To be informed. You must tell them what you are doing with their data.
02
To access. You must provide their data within 7 days.
03
To object. You must stop processing, or explain why you cannot, within 14 days.
04
To rectification. You must correct inaccurate data within 14 days.
05
To erasure. You must delete data you no longer lawfully hold within 14 days.
06
To data portability. You must transmit their data in a machine readable format within 30 days.
07
Not to be subject to automated decision making. You must notify them and allow human review.
Data Protection Act, 2019 and the Data Protection
(General) Regulations, 2021.

Roles
Know who you
are under
the law.
A
Controller. Determines why and how personal data is processed. Carries primary legal responsibility.
B
Processor. Processes data on behalf of the controller. Liable only if acting outside instructions.
C
Data subject. The person whose data is processed. Holds the rights the law exists to protect.
